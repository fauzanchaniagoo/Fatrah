## Brand & Style

The brand visual identity bridges the Maria Montessori philosophy of the "prepared environment"—order, beauty, simplicity, and tactile accessibility—with Islamic values of intentionality, tranquility (*sakinah*), and wholesome natural disposition (*fitrah*). 

The target audience comprises thoughtful parents, educators, and young families seeking an intentional, screen-balanced, child-centered educational compass. The user experience must evoke calm confidence, unhurried warmth, visual spaciousness, and organic clarity.

The visual style blends **Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile Softness**:
- **Uncluttered whitespace:** generous margins echoing an ordered Montessori classroom shelf.
- **Organic balance:** earthy, soothing teals paired with lush verdant tones, grounded on warm cream surfaces rather than harsh stark whites.
- **Subtle spiritual geometry:** modular grid alignments with delicate 8-fold and 12-fold geometric watermarks or dividing lines at very low opacity (3–5%).
- **Harmonious pacing:** soft, child-accessible shapes with refined editorial elegance for parents.

## Colors

The palette derives from natural Mediterranean and Middle Eastern landscapes: deep water, fragrant cypress, olive groves, and sunlight on pale stone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Deep Teal (`#084D68`):** The foundational anchor for high-priority actions, primary navigational headers, and critical emphasis. Conveys grounded institutional trust and contemplative depth.
- **Secondary Teal (`#0D7685`):** Expressive interactive state for links, secondary callouts, and active tabs.
- **Vibrant Sage (`#69C181`):** The nurturing tone for learning progress, milestones completed, organic badges, and verified accomplishments.
- **Soft Pistachio Accent (`#CCF186`):** Reserved for energetic highlight tags, new module pills, interactive touch-points, and notification accents. Always paired with `#084D68` or `#1E293B` text to ensure WCAG AAA contrast compliance.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: Warm Linen Cream (`#FBFBFA`).
  - Elevated Container: Pure Clean White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Recessed / Inset Surface: Whispering Sage-Cream (`#F4F6F4`).
- **Text & Neutral Slate:**
  - High Emphasis: Deep Slate Charcoal (`#1E293B`).
  - Medium Emphasis / Body: Muted Slate (`#334155`).
  - Low Emphasis / Captions: Soft Slate Grey (`#64748B`).
  - Hairline Borders: Soft Stone (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The type scale pairs an organic, dignified serif (`Playfair Display`) with an exceptionally legible, friendly geometric sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). 

- **Display & Section Headers (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for high-level narrative introductions, module titles, and reflection quotes. The editorial serif evokes the timeless tradition of classical scholarship, literature, and quiet beauty.
- **Interface, Functional Content & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clarity and warm accessibility across reading prompts, observation charts, parental guides, and child activity instructions.
- **RTL & Script Harmony:** When rendering Quranic ayaat, Hadith citations, or Arabic nomenclature, preserve line heights at 1.75x to prevent diacritic clipping, pairing with an aligned traditional Naskh or Amiri font face.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an intentional, orderly grid inspired by the zoned organization of a Montessori classroom.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, maximum content container width of `1280px`, outer margins of `2.5rem`, and `1.5rem` gutters. Elements span modular multiples: 3 columns for curriculum item cards, 4 columns for parent resources, and 6–8 columns for reading material.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.75rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Touch targets maintain a minimum vertical clearance of `48px`.

Generous vertical pacing (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) prevents cognitive overload, giving each activity and parental reflection ample breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh artificial drop shadows, adopting **Tonal Layering with Whispering Ambient Shadows**:

1. **Flat Base Canvas:** Surface level 0 (`#FBFBFA`) provides a calm, neutral canvas.
2. **Layered Surfaces (Level 1):** Activity cards and main containers sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a low-contrast border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, warm teal-tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(8, 77, 104, 0.04)`.
3. **Elevated & Interactive (Level 2):** Hovered cards, flyouts, and dialogs rise with soft double diffusion: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(8, 77, 104, 0.07), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(8, 77, 104, 0.03)`.
4. **Recessed Wells:** Input fields, observational notes, and interactive child sandboxes utilize sunken tonal backgrounds (`#F4F6F4`) with a soft internal boundary instead of heavy inner shadows.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape aesthetic, reflecting smooth wooden Montessori materials (like cylinder blocks and sensorial curves) and nature-inspired geometry.

- Standard interactive components (cards, text inputs): `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px).
- Modals, large activity cards, and thematic containers: `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`).
- Micro-elements, status indicators, and interactive CTAs: fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for a soft, tactile feel.
- Pure geometric icons and badges are framed in smoothed squircle or circular containment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#084D68`, text `#FFFFFF`, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover shifts to `#0D7685` with subtle elevation. Active press scales slightly down (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#F4F6F4`, text `#084D68`, border `1px solid #0D7685/20`. Hover shifts background to `#E5EAE5`.
- **Tertiary / Accent Pill:** Background `#CCF186`, text `#084D68`, font weight 600. Used for featured action triggers.

### Cards & Learning Shelves
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-xl` corners and soft `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- Header sections within cards may feature gentle top-edge accent strips in `#69C181` or `#CCF186` (3px thickness).
- Generous internal padding (`1.5rem` to `2rem`).

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped status chips for development stages (e.g., *Infant 0-3*, *Practical Life*, *Sensorial*, *Tawhid & Nature*).
- Background colors use muted tinting: Sage background (`#69C181/15`) with text `#0D7685`, or Soft Pistachio (`#CCF186`) with text `#084D68`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Height of 48px to support easy finger tap targets.
- Background `#F4F6F4`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, corners `rounded-lg` (`0.75rem`).
- Active focus state: border transitions smoothly to `#0D7685` with a subtle focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 118, 133, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes feature rounded corners (`0.375rem`), with active check state filled in `#084D68` and an off-white checkmark.
- Radio buttons feature concentric rings: `#084D68` outer rim, `#FFFFFF` separator gap, and an inner solid dot of `#69C181`.

### Montessori Progress Rails
- Smooth, rounded bar indicator with track `#F4F6F4` and fill in `#69C181` moving to `#0D7685`.
- Completed stage nodes highlighted by a small floral or 8-point geometric check icon.

### Reflection & Quranic Quotation Callouts
- Warm container with `#FBFBFA` background, left border (4px) in `#084D68`, and an ultra-faint watermark of subtle Islamic geometry in the top-right corner.
- Body formatted in `Playfair Display` italic with body text in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
